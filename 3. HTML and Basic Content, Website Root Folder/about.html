About NovaTech Computer Solutions

NovaTech Computer Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a small information technology business based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. The company was established to provide reliable and affordable technology products and IT support to students, home users, schools and small businesses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide dependable, affordable and innovative IT solutions that improve the daily lives of our customers through quality products and professional technical support.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the most trusted and customer-focused technology solution providers in South Africa by delivering reliable services and embracing new technologies.

Our Core Values

  • Reliability
  • Professionalism
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Affordability
  • Innovation
  • Integrity

Who We Serve

NovaTech serves students, home users, educational institutions and small to medium-sized businesses that require computer products, technical assistance and dependable IT solutions.
